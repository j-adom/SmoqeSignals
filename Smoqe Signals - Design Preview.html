Meet the pitmaster

Shon, the man behind the smoke

Smoqe Signals is built on hospitality, patience, and West Tennessee roots that run back to Paris, TN and two uncles named Clyde and Clay. Shon brings that standard to the truck, the pit, and every catered table.

The menu stays grounded in the classics: smoke-kissed meats, sturdy sides, and food that makes an event feel personal instead of packaged.
